Common Questions

Things people ask
before we start.

Timelines vary by channel. Paid media campaigns typically generate meaningful data and early ROAS signals within the first 30–45 days. SEO and organic growth strategies build momentum over 3–6 months, with compounding returns thereafter. We set clear KPI benchmarks at the start of every engagement so expectations are always aligned.

We work with brands at multiple stages, but we're most effective with companies that have achieved product-market fit and are ready to scale aggressively. For early-stage brands, we can help build the digital foundations — tracking infrastructure, website performance, and an initial acquisition strategy — to set you up for scalable growth.

We begin every new partnership with a comprehensive audit covering your current digital performance, tracking health, competitive landscape, and growth opportunities. From that, we produce a 90-day strategic roadmap. You'll have a dedicated account lead, bi-weekly strategy calls, and a live performance dashboard updated in real time.

Transparency is non-negotiable for us. Every client receives access to a custom Looker Studio dashboard that consolidates all channel performance in one view — ad spend, revenue, ROAS, SEO rankings, conversion rates, and more. We send a concise weekly performance digest and conduct a deep-dive monthly review call with your team.

For paid acquisition, we recommend a minimum monthly ad spend of €2,000–€3,000 on a single channel to generate statistically significant data within the learning phase. For multi-channel strategies, we build a budget recommendation based on your growth targets, margins, and competitive context. Quality of setup always outperforms raw spend volume.
